# Widgets you can answer, a Dynamic Island study timer, and a schedule fitted to you

**Install or update:** open SideStore → Memory Garden → **Update**. New here? Follow the step-by-step guide: https://mxhddd.github.io/Memory-App/

## New
- **Answer flashcards from the Home Screen and Lock Screen.** Show the answer, then tap Again or Got it, without opening the app.
- **Live Activity and Dynamic Island.** Today's trail on the Lock Screen, and a study countdown that keeps ticking when you leave the app.
- **Control Center buttons** for Start training, Review a card and Done on the next reminder. They can go on the Lock Screen or the Action button too.
- **Siri and Shortcuts:** "What's next in Memory Garden", "Start training", "Add a reminder".
- **Answer a card from a notification** by typing the answer straight into it.
- **A schedule fitted to you.** After 50 reviews the Vault learns how quickly *you* forget, shows how much you really remembered, and suggests the most efficient target.
- **Knowing what you know.** Guess your score before recall; Progress shows how close you get.
- **Study speed ladder.** The study clock tightens only once your accuracy holds.
- **New screens:** the grounds (Train), a deck shelf (Vault), a day path (Reminders), themed Settings.
- **Refresh reminders.** The app tells you two days before the free signature runs out.

## Fixed
31 bugs, including alarms that kept ringing after Delete everything, Today showing yesterday's plan after midnight, the screen locking mid-drill, and right and wrong answers looking alike in the Atelier look.

Requires iOS 18 or later.
